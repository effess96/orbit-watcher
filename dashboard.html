Watches

Costs, minimum profit and fees are your assumptions. Saving restarts the watcher; gaps open at that moment are dropped, not mistimed.

New-pool hunter

Checks the newest Solana pools every 5 minutes. When a new token already has 2+ usable pools (each above $10k), it is watched automatically for 30 minutes (max 3 at once). This is where ANB-type events happen.

Alerts

Telegram alerts for gaps that pass the depth check and for single-update moves of 3%+. Set TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN and TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID in Railway to turn them on.
